Host guide

Share your space, earn extra income.

Everything from signing up to getting paid. Free to list, zero commission — you keep everything guests pay. Most hosts finish their first listing in about 10 minutes.

Getting started

Anyone with a great space can host — event halls, studios, meeting rooms, co-working spots, photoshoot locations, and more.

  1. Create an account at fiilar.com — name, email, and password, or one tap with Continue with Google.
  2. Verify your email — we send a code straight to your inbox.
  3. Choose “Host your space” and add your phone number. That's it — you're a host, and listing is completely free.
Zero commission. Fiilar doesn't take a cut of your bookings. The price you set is the money you receive.

Verify your identity

Before your first listing goes live, we confirm you're a real person or a real business — it keeps every guest and host on Fiilar safe. You only do this once.

Use any of these

  • Personal ID — international passport, national ID, driver's licence, or voter's card. You'll take a quick selfie and verification is usually instant.
  • CAC certificate — registered businesses can verify with their business registration instead. No selfie needed; our team reviews it, usually the same day.

Create your listing

From your host dashboard, choose Start your listing. The steps are Basics → Stand Out → Pricing → Policies → Publish, and your progress saves automatically — leave anytime and pick up where you stopped.

Basics — what and where

  • Pick your space type, give it a clear title and an honest, inviting description.
  • Set the location and how many guests it holds.

Too busy to write? Let Fiilar draft it

Tap “Write it for me”, then paste rough notes, an Instagram caption — or upload your existing brochure (PDF or Word). Fiilar drafts the title, description, amenities, and more from what you give it. You review and edit everything before it's saved.

Stand Out — photos sell the space

  • Add at least 5 photos — bright, landscape, shot in daylight. Lead with your best wide shot.
  • Select your amenities (Wi-Fi, parking, AC, power backup…) and tags so the right guests find you.

Publish — live within 24 hours

Submit your listing and our team reviews it — usually live within 24 hours. You'll get a notification the moment it's approved.

Pricing & availability

  • Rate — charge per hour or per day, in naira. Check what similar spaces near you charge and start competitive.
  • Caution fee (optional) — a refundable deposit guests pay alongside the booking, returned after a damage-free session.
  • Opening hours — the days and times guests can book.
  • Buffer time — a gap between bookings so you can reset the space.
  • Minimum duration & booking window — the shortest booking you'll accept and how far ahead guests can book.

Policies & house rules

Cancellation policy

Choose how refunds work when a guest cancels:

  • Moderate (recommended) — guests get a full refund if they cancel at least 5 days before the booking. No refund after that.
  • Strict — 50% refund if cancelled at least 7 days before. No refund after that.
  • Non-refundable — no refunds once the booking is confirmed.

House rules

Say clearly what's allowed — noise, smoking, decorations, cleanup expectations. Guests see and accept your rules before they book. Clear rules prevent awkward conversations later.

Your public page

Every host gets a beautiful public page at fiilar.com/h/yourname showing your brand and all your spaces. It's your link-in-bio and your business card in one.

  1. In your host dashboard, open My Page.
  2. Choose your handle (the name in your link), business name, and tagline.
  3. Copy your link and share it on WhatsApp, Instagram, anywhere — clients can browse and book directly from it.

Bookings & check-in

  • New bookings appear on your dashboard and in Bookings — you'll also get a notification.
  • Instant book — if enabled, confirmed guests book without waiting for you. Otherwise you approve each request.
  • Arrivals today — the dashboard shows who's arriving each day so nothing catches you off guard.
  • Check-in — when the guest arrives, open their booking to confirm their details and check them in.

Messages

Guests message you from the listing or their booking — replies show in your Messages with the booking details right beside the conversation. Fast replies win bookings: guests book hosts who answer.

Stay on Fiilar. Keep conversations and payments on the platform — it's what protects both you and the guest if anything goes wrong.

Earnings & payouts

  1. Guests pay through Fiilar when they book — you never have to chase payment.
  2. The money is held securely while the booking runs, then added to your earnings after the session completes.
  3. Add your bank account in Earnings & Payouts, and withdraw to it whenever you like.

Your Earnings page shows every booking, what's pending, and what's available to withdraw.
