Guest guide

Find the space. Book it in minutes.

Event halls, studios, meeting rooms, photoshoot locations — how to find them, book them, and pay safely on Fiilar.

On this page

  • Booking
  • Payments & protection

1 · Finding a space

  • Browse or search on fiilar.com — by city, category, or what you're planning.
  • Open a listing to see photos, amenities, exact capacity, reviews from past guests, and the host's profile.
  • Save your favourites with the heart — sign in and they're kept for you under Saved.

2 · Booking

  1. Pick your date and time — spaces are booked by the hour or by the day. You'll see exactly what's available.
  2. Add your guest count and any extra services the host offers.
  3. Review the full price — everything is itemised before you pay, and you can apply a promo code. You won't be charged until you confirm at checkout.
  4. Confirm and pay. Some spaces confirm instantly; for others the host approves your request first — you'll be notified either way.
Some spaces ask for ID verification before booking — a quick one-time identity check that keeps hosts and guests safe. You'll be guided through it if it's needed.

3 · Payments & protection

  • Pay by card or your Fiilar wallet — payments are processed securely.
  • Your money is protected — Fiilar holds the payment and only releases it to the host after your session completes.
  • Caution fee — some listings include a refundable deposit, returned to you after a damage-free session.
  • Always pay through Fiilar. Never transfer money to a host directly or move the conversation off the platform — that's what makes your booking protected.

4 · Cancellations & refunds

Every listing shows its cancellation policy before you book — on the listing page and again at checkout. The policies:

  • Moderate — full refund if you cancel at least 5 days before your booking starts. No refund after that.
  • Strict — 50% refund if you cancel at least 7 days before. No refund after that.
  • Non-refundable — no refund once the booking is confirmed.

Refunds go back to your original payment method or Fiilar wallet.

5 · Check-in day

  • Your booking has everything — address, time, and the house rules you accepted. Find it under Bookings.
  • Arrive on time — the host confirms your booking and checks you in.
  • Message the host from your booking if you're running late or can't find the place.
  • Respect the space and the rules — it keeps your caution fee fully refundable and your reviews glowing.

Something not right? Tell us.

Email info@fiilar.com with your booking details and we'll sort it out with you.
